Why Hardly Notable exists

The work is technology.
The reason is personal.

Hardly Notable was founded in memory of family whose lives continue to shape the standards behind the work: show up, make something meaningful, and leave more behind than you found.

Sawyer and Cowboy are a big part of why I started Hardly Notable. They inspired me to build a life with more freedom—one where I could give them the very best, be present for the ordinary moments, and spend more time with the ones who matter most. They are not what we build; they are part of the reason I chose to build something of my own.

Hardly Notable is my way of creating that life while building something meaningful enough to endure beyond any one moment—or any one lifetime.
