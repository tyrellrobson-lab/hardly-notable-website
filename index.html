The studio

Independent by design.

Hardly Notable is an independent game and software studio focused on turning simple ideas into products people want to keep using.

Sometimes those ideas are ours. Sometimes they’re yours. Either way, the goal is the same: thoughtful work, a clear point of view, and fewer features that exist solely because somebody scheduled a meeting.
