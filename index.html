Questions

Good questions to ask before hiring anyone.

Don't see yours? Email hello@hardlynotable.com and you'll get a reply from a person.

How much does a website cost?

$299 for a one-page website, flat. You pay $150 to start and $149 when you approve the preview, before it goes live. Hosting and domain fees are separate and agreed up front.

What does the $75 basic app include?

One simple tool with one core feature, such as a quote calculator, checklist, sign-up form or job tracker, that runs in a web browser on phone and desktop, with your branding and one round of revisions. It doesn't include App Store publishing, logins, payments, saving data to a server, connections to other software or ongoing support. Those are quoted separately.

Do I have to pay every month?

No. The $25-a-month care plan for hosting, small edits and upkeep is optional.

How long does it take?

About five business days after we receive the deposit, your content and any access we need. We agree your actual start date first.

Who owns the website?

You do. We set it up on hosting you control and hand over the files.

Do I need to write everything myself?

You supply your text, logo and photos, and we shape them into a page that reads well. Extensive copywriting is quoted separately.

Can we talk before I decide?

Yes. Book a free 15-minute call or send a short inquiry. There's no deposit or commitment to ask questions.

What's next for your business?

Tell us what you need. A real person usually replies within one business day.
