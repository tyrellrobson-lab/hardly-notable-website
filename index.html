Before you ask

A few useful answers.

Do I need a finished plan before I reach out?

No. A rough idea, a problem worth solving, or a product you need improved is enough. Defining the useful version is part of the work.

What kinds of clients are a good fit?

Founders, small businesses, and teams that need a focused digital product and value direct collaboration, clear decisions, and practical scope.

Can Hardly Notable improve something that already exists?

Yes. We can review an existing app or website, identify the highest-value improvements, and scope a focused redesign or rebuild.

What happens after I submit a project brief?

Your brief goes directly to the studio. We review the idea, follow up with the most useful questions, and determine whether the project and timing are a fit.
