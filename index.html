Why Hardly Notable exists

The work is technology.
The reason is personal.

Hardly Notable was founded in memory of family whose lives continue to shape the standards behind the work: show up, make something meaningful, and leave more behind than you found.

Hardly Notable began with Sawyer and Cowboy—and the hope of building something that could carry their joy, personality, and legacy forward.

I started HN for them. I wanted the things we create to keep a piece of who they are in the world, even long after I am gone. They are not the product. They are the heart behind why the products exist.
