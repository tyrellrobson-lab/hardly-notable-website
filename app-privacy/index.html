App Privacy

How the current Hardly Notable app builds handle information.

Updated November 27, 2026. This policy covers the approved release versions, the versions submitted for release and the reviewed development builds identified below. Approval or submission does not itself establish public availability. Disclosures will be reviewed before later releases or changes to data handling.

Products covered

This policy covers Night Shift Sort 1.2.0, Critter Clutter 1.3.0, Sandglint 1.2.0, Recall: Oddity Match 1.3.0, and earlier released versions of these games. Coverage does not mean every build is publicly available on both stores. These games do not include paid ads or in-app purchases. On the App Store, Critter Clutter and Night Shift Sort are labeled Data Not Collected. Sandglint discloses the email address and message you choose to send when contacting support.

The Android builds covered by this policy handle information in the same way: progress and settings stay on the device, with no account, paid advertising, analytics or gameplay uploads. If a player has Android backup turned on, Android may copy app data to their Google account under Google's own policies.

It also covers the reviewed development and private-test builds of Match/Decision and Brain Chaos. These development builds are not presented as publicly released.

Hardly Notable also has future products in development. Those products are not presented as publicly available, and their disclosures will be confirmed before release.

Product Lens is a separate website tool. Its handling of ideas, optional handoffs, and any AI processing is described in the Product Lens privacy details.

Current data practices

The reviewed builds do not use advertising networks, analytics services, tracking technologies, or user accounts. Hardly Notable LLC does not sell personal information or use app activity to track people across apps or websites.

The latest builds of Critter Clutter, Night Shift Sort and Sandglint include “More from Hardly Notable,” a list of our other games built into the app. The list loads without contacting a server. If you choose a link, it opens the game’s App Store or Google Play page. Critter Clutter asks for a grown-up check first. The links do not include tracking identifiers or send your gameplay data to the store. Apple or Google handles activity on its store under its own privacy policy.

Information stored on your device

Some apps store game progress, scores, unlocked rewards, preferences, reminders, lists, or other user-entered content locally on the device so the app can remember the experience. Removing an app may remove its locally stored information.

Critter Clutter

Critter Clutter section updated November 27, 2026. The latest Critter Clutter 1.3.0 build saves level progress, stars and your Sound, Haptics and Reduce Motion settings on your device. This includes stars for later levels in the continuous numbered sequence. Saved data from earlier builds, including older Endless or Codes records, can remain on your device. The game does not require an account and has no paid ads, purchases, analytics or gameplay-data uploads. Its sound effects, including the animal calls, play locally; it does not record audio or request microphone access.

Uninstalling the app removes its local app data. Device backup services may retain their own copies.

Night Shift Sort

Night Shift Sort section updated November 27, 2026. The latest Night Shift Sort 1.2.0 build saves numbered level progress, stars, earned one-ups and sound, touch feedback and Reduce Motion settings on your device. It also stores progress in the separate Endless mode, including wins and recently played shift codes, and whether you have seen the “New: Endless” notice. Saved data from earlier builds, such as coins and records, can remain on your device. The game does not require an account and has no paid ads, purchases, analytics or gameplay-data uploads. If you share a shift code, you choose where to send it; the game does not send it automatically. Sound effects play locally; the game does not record audio or request microphone access.

Uninstalling the app removes its local app data. Web-preview progress is stored in the browser’s site storage and can be removed through the browser’s site-data controls. Device or browser backup services may retain their own copies. Browser previews also contact the server hosting the preview; this is separate from uploading gameplay data.

Sandglint

Sandglint section updated November 27, 2026. The latest Sandglint 1.2.0 build saves your level grades, including levels after Book 2, your settings and your current Book 1 or Book 2 puzzle on your device. It also keeps a previous Book 1 or Book 2 save for recovery. This build no longer offers the Codes feature; older Endless or Codes data can still remain on your device. The game does not require an account and has no paid ads, in-app purchases, analytics SDK, cloud saves or automatic gameplay uploads. Sound effects play locally; the game does not record audio or request microphone access. Device backup settings may include local app data. Settings > Erase progress resets current grades and the active puzzle. Uninstalling removes the game’s local data.

Send feedback opens your email app with the game's name and version. You choose what to write and whether to send. If you send an email, Hardly Notable receives your sender address and message to handle your feedback or respond. No screenshots, gameplay logs or device identifiers are attached automatically. Your email provider processes the email under its own policies. Copy email address writes only our public support address to your clipboard when you tap it; the game does not read clipboard contents.

Support email retention policy adopted November 1, 2026: We keep Sandglint feedback emails while handling the issue, then delete them within 90 days after resolution. You can request deletion by emailing hardlynotablellc@gmail.com.

For Sandglint support, privacy questions or a request concerning feedback you emailed us, contact hardlynotablellc@gmail.com. Please avoid including sensitive personal information.

Recall: Oddity Match

Recall section updated November 18, 2026. Recall: Oddity Match 1.3.0 stores game progress, rewards, bests, and Haptics and Reduce Motion preferences locally on your device. It does not require an account and has no advertising, purchases, analytics, tracking, or online gameplay. It has no sound and does not record audio or request microphone access.

Settings > Support > Email Support opens your email app with the subject “Recall Support”. You choose what to write and whether to send. If you send an email, Hardly Notable receives your sender address and message to respond. Uninstalling the app removes its local app data. Device backup services may retain their own copies.

Match/Decision — Two Phones mode

Same Phone mode operates entirely on the device. Two Phones mode sends an anonymous temporary session code, anonymous seat credentials, the shared choice deck, and each participant’s choices to Hardly Notable’s service over HTTPS so the app can calculate mutual matches. The service does not require a name, email address, phone number, account, contact list, or precise location.

Individual choices are erased after the match is calculated or the session is cancelled. Incomplete sessions expire after two hours. Hosting-provider security logs and backups may have separate limited retention. This temporary session information is used only to provide the matching feature; it is not sold, used for advertising, or used for tracking.

Photos and device features

If a future released app lets a user select a photo or use another device feature, access will occur only after the user chooses that feature and grants the permission requested by the operating system. The applicable app listing and this policy will be updated before release to describe any such handling.

Support messages

If you choose to email support, your email service sends the message and any attachments you include. Share only information needed to explain the issue. The apps do not send support messages automatically.

Children’s privacy

The reviewed builds do not knowingly collect personal information from children. Match/Decision’s Two Phones mode should not be used to submit personal information.

Retention and deletion

Locally stored information remains until it is removed by the user, the app, or the operating system. Match/Decision users can cancel an active Two Phones session; individual choices are erased after matching or cancellation, and incomplete sessions expire after two hours.

Future features

Before any app adds advertising, analytics, purchases, accounts, cloud storage, or other functionality that changes data handling, its disclosures and this policy will be reviewed and updated.
