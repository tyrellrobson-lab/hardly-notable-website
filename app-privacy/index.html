App Privacy

How the current Hardly Notable app builds handle information.

Updated October 30, 2026. This policy covers the approved release versions and reviewed development builds identified below. Approval does not itself establish public availability. Disclosures will be reviewed before later releases or changes to data handling.

Products covered

This policy covers Critter Clutter 1.0.0 (build 8) and Night Shift Sort 1.1.0 (build 14), approved for release. Both approved versions have App Store privacy disclosures of Data Not Collected, with no ads or in-app purchases.

It also covers the reviewed development and private-test builds of Recall, Match/Decision and Brain Chaos. These development builds are not presented as publicly released.

Hardly Notable also has future products in development. Those products are not presented as publicly available, and their disclosures will be confirmed before release.

Product Lens is a separate website tool. Its handling of ideas, optional handoffs, and any AI processing is described in the Product Lens privacy details.

Current data practices

The reviewed builds do not use advertising networks, analytics services, tracking technologies, or user accounts. Hardly Notable LLC does not sell personal information or use app activity to track people across apps or websites.

Information stored on your device

Some apps store game progress, scores, unlocked rewards, preferences, reminders, lists, or other user-entered content locally on the device so the app can remember the experience. Removing an app may remove its locally stored information.

Night Shift Sort

Night Shift Sort section updated November 5, 2026. Night Shift Sort 1.1.0 (build 14) stores level progress, stars, and sound, touch feedback, and Reduce Motion preferences locally on your device. Saved data from earlier versions, such as coins and records, stays on the device. It does not require an account and has no active advertising, purchases, analytics, or gameplay-data uploads. Its sound effects play locally; this build does not record audio or request microphone access.

Uninstalling the app removes its local app data. Web-preview progress is stored in the browser’s site storage and can be removed through the browser’s site-data controls. Device or browser backup services may retain their own copies. Browser previews also contact the server hosting the preview; this is separate from uploading gameplay data.

Sandglint

Sandglint section updated November 3, 2026. Sandglint stores your level grades, preferences and current puzzle on your device, with a previous-save backup for recovery. It has no sign-in, advertising, analytics SDK, in-app purchases, cloud save or automatic gameplay upload. Device backup settings may include local app data. Settings > Erase progress removes your grades and current puzzle from the game.

Send feedback opens your email app with the game's name and version. You choose what to write and whether to send. If you send an email, Hardly Notable receives your sender address and message to handle your feedback or respond. No screenshots, gameplay logs or device identifiers are attached automatically. Your email provider processes the email under its own policies. Copy email address writes only our public support address to your clipboard when you tap it; the game does not read clipboard contents.

Support email retention policy adopted November 1, 2026: We keep Sandglint feedback emails while handling the issue, then delete them within 90 days after resolution. You can request deletion by emailing hardlynotablellc@gmail.com.

For Sandglint support, privacy questions or a request concerning feedback you emailed us, contact hardlynotablellc@gmail.com. Please avoid including sensitive personal information.

Match/Decision — Two Phones mode

Same Phone mode operates entirely on the device. Two Phones mode sends an anonymous temporary session code, anonymous seat credentials, the shared choice deck, and each participant’s choices to Hardly Notable’s service over HTTPS so the app can calculate mutual matches. The service does not require a name, email address, phone number, account, contact list, or precise location.

Individual choices are erased after the match is calculated or the session is cancelled. Incomplete sessions expire after two hours. Hosting-provider security logs and backups may have separate limited retention. This temporary session information is used only to provide the matching feature; it is not sold, used for advertising, or used for tracking.

Photos and device features

If a future released app lets a user select a photo or use another device feature, access will occur only after the user chooses that feature and grants the permission requested by the operating system. The applicable app listing and this policy will be updated before release to describe any such handling.

Support messages

If you choose to email support, your email service sends the message and any attachments you include. Share only information needed to explain the issue. The apps do not send support messages automatically.

Children’s privacy

The reviewed builds do not knowingly collect personal information from children. Match/Decision’s Two Phones mode should not be used to submit personal information.

Retention and deletion

Locally stored information remains until it is removed by the user, the app, or the operating system. Match/Decision users can cancel an active Two Phones session; individual choices are erased after matching or cancellation, and incomplete sessions expire after two hours.

Future features

Before any app adds advertising, analytics, purchases, accounts, cloud storage, or other functionality that changes data handling, its disclosures and this policy will be reviewed and updated.
