App Privacy

Effective September 8, 2026

This policy covers Recall: Oddity Match, Critter Clutter, Perfect Timing, Last Time, Brain Chaos, Match / Decision, Micro-Party, Subscription Tracker, and Photo Paint-by-Number, published or being developed by Hardly Notable LLC.

Current data practices

The currently reviewed builds do not collect, transmit, sell, or share personal information. They do not contain user accounts, advertising networks, analytics services, tracking technologies, or cloud backends.

Information stored on your device

Some apps store game progress, scores, unlocked rewards, preferences, reminders, lists, or other user-entered content locally on the device. This information is not automatically transmitted to Hardly Notable LLC. Removing an app may remove its locally stored information.

Photos and device features

If a future released app lets a user select a photo or use another device feature, access will occur only after the user chooses that feature and grants permission. The applicable disclosures will be updated before release.

Children’s privacy

The reviewed builds do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Retention and deletion

Hardly Notable LLC does not retain personal or gameplay data from the reviewed builds. Locally stored information remains until removed by the user, the app, or the operating system.

Future features

Before any app adds advertising, analytics, purchases, accounts, cloud storage, or other functionality that changes data handling, its disclosures and this policy will be reviewed and updated.

Contact

Questions may be sent to support@hardlynotable.com.
