A straightforward process

Agree. Build.
Review. Launch.

  1. Agree the scope and start date. We confirm content, hosting, ownership, acceptance and cancellation/refund terms in your project agreement.

Hosting, domain registration and third-party fees are paid directly by you and agreed before commitment. Additional pages, extensive copywriting, branding, photography, online stores, payments, customer accounts, custom booking systems, AI features and ongoing updates are separately scoped.

Changes to the agreed scope are quoted before work starts. The package does not promise search rankings or sales results.
