How it works

Send. Test.
Fix. Re-check.

  1. Tell us about your app. We agree what to test: up to 10 things a user must be able to do.
  2. Pay up front ($99, or $49 at the founding price), then send a TestFlight invite or the App Store link, plus a test account if the app needs sign-in.
  3. Get your report within 3 business days. Ranked by what to fix first, with steps to reproduce.
  4. Fix, then we re-check. One re-check of your fixes within 14 days is included. A new version later is $39.

What isn’t included?

Android, automated test scripts, load or security testing, and fixing the code. We report what we find; we don’t change your app.

A launch check finds problems a careful person would hit. It does not promise App Store approval or that no bugs remain.
