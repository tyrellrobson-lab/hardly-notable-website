Privacy Policy for Hardly Notable Writing

Effective date: September 8, 2026

Hardly Notable Writing is published by Hardly Notable LLC.

This Privacy Policy explains what information Hardly Notable Writing handles, how that information is used, and how users can contact us about privacy questions.

Information stored on your device

Hardly Notable Writing stores certain preferences and reading information locally on your device so the app can remember your experience.

This may include:

  • favorite or bookmarked writing
  • reading progress
  • your most recently read piece
  • reader preferences such as text size and reading theme
  • temporary submission-draft information, if that feature is used

This information is intended to remain on your device and is not automatically sent to Hardly Notable LLC.

Writing submissions

Hardly Notable Writing includes an optional feature that allows users to submit original writing for possible publication.

If you choose to submit writing, you may provide:

  • your name
  • your email address
  • the title and category of your work
  • the writing you submit
  • an optional message to the editor

Submitting writing does not guarantee publication.

You retain ownership of your original work. By submitting it, you give Hardly Notable LLC permission to review the submission for possible publication. Any additional permission required for publication may be addressed with the contributor separately.

Submissions are sent through the email application or email service you choose to use. Hardly Notable Writing does not silently transmit submissions in the background.

How submission information is used

Submission information is used only for purposes reasonably related to editorial review, communication with the contributor, potential publication, and administration of accepted work.

Hardly Notable LLC does not sell submitted personal information or writing.

Retention and deletion

Unpublished submissions may be retained for editorial review for up to 12 months.

You may request deletion of an unpublished submission sooner by contacting:

support@hardlynotable.com

If a piece is accepted for publication, Hardly Notable LLC may retain the submission and necessary contact or publication records for as long as reasonably necessary to publish and administer that work.

Third-party services

Hardly Notable Writing may rely on services provided by your device or operating system, such as your chosen email application, when you initiate a writing submission or open an external link.

Those third-party services operate under their own privacy policies.

Hardly Notable Writing does not currently use advertising networks or require user accounts.

Children

Hardly Notable Writing is not designed as a service specifically directed to children. Users should not submit another person’s personal information without appropriate permission.

Security

Hardly Notable LLC takes reasonable steps to limit the information collected by the app and to use personal information only for the purposes described in this policy.

No method of electronic transmission or storage can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

Changes to this policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time. The current version will be available at:

hardlynotable.com/privacy

The effective date above will be updated when material changes are made.

Contact

For privacy questions, deletion requests, or support relating to this policy:
