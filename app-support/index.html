Product Support

Support information for Hardly Notable apps and games.

Released apps

Support covers the released versions of Critter Clutter and Night Shift Sort, available as free apps in the United States, with no ads or in-app purchases. The current released versions are Critter Clutter 1.1.0 and Night Shift Sort 1.1.0; the App Store listing and your installed app show which version is currently available to you. Please include your installed version and build number when contacting support.

Development and private-test builds

Support also covers Recall, Match/Decision, Brain Chaos, and separately identified development or private-test builds. These development builds are not presented as publicly released.

Before writing

  1. Close and reopen the app.
  2. Check that the operating system and app are current.
  3. Do not uninstall if you want to preserve locally stored progress.
  4. Try the action again and note exactly where it stopped working.

Critter Clutter support

Tap a pen to send its front herd onto the road, and each animal walks to the home made for its kind. Levels unlock in order across 10 worlds of 50 levels each. Players of earlier versions keep their settings and start the new levels at level 1. Sound, Haptics, and Reduce Motion are in Settings, and Sound follows your iPhone’s silent switch.

Include the app version and build number, your device, the level number, and the last action before the problem. Progress is stored locally, so avoid uninstalling the app while troubleshooting if you want to keep it.

Night Shift Sort support

Include the app version and build number, your device, the level number, and the last action before the problem. Mention whether you were using the installed app or a browser preview. Progress and preferences are stored locally, so avoid uninstalling the app or clearing browser site data while troubleshooting if you want to keep them.

Sound starts on and follows your iPhone’s silent switch. If you expect to hear effects, check the silent switch and the game’s Sound setting; Sound, Touch feedback, and Reduce Motion are separate settings.

Sandglint

Route glass pipes from the spout to the matching collector across 24 levels, then pour. Drag a piece from the tray onto the board, and tap a selected piece to turn it. Trickle tests a route with a little sand, Clear removes the sand and keeps your pipes, and Undo steps back. Levels unlock in order, and a Hint appears after a failed or stuck pour. Sound, touch feedback, reduced effects and button controls are in Settings. Sound follows your phone's silent switch.

For help, ideas or bug reports, email hardlynotablellc@gmail.com, or use Settings > Send feedback, which opens an email draft for you to review and send. Nothing is sent automatically. Please avoid passwords or sensitive personal information.

What to include

Please include the product name, what happened, what you expected, your device model, operating-system version, app version and build number, and any useful level, room, or session number. A screenshot is welcome, provided it does not show information you do not want to share.
