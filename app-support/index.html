Before writing

  1. Close and reopen the app.
  2. Check that the operating system and app are current.
  3. Do not uninstall if you want to preserve locally stored progress.
  4. Try the action again and note exactly where it stopped working.

What to include

Please include the product name, what happened, what you expected, your phone model, operating-system version, and any useful level, room, or session number. A screenshot is welcome, provided it does not show information you do not want to share.
