Product Support

Support information for Hardly Notable apps and games.

Current product group

Support is available for the following development and private-test builds:

  • Recall
  • Match/Decision
  • Brain Chaos
  • Critter Clutter
  • Night Shift Sort

These products remain in development or private testing and are not publicly released. Public availability and official store links will be added only after release.

Before writing

  1. Close and reopen the app.
  2. Check that the operating system and app are current.
  3. Do not uninstall if you want to preserve locally stored progress.
  4. Try the action again and note exactly where it stopped working.

Night Shift Sort testers

Include the app version and build number, your device, the level or night, and the last action before the problem. Mention whether you were using the installed app or a browser preview. Progress and preferences are stored locally, so avoid uninstalling the app or clearing browser site data while troubleshooting if you want to keep them.

Sound starts off by default. Check the game’s sound preference if you expect to hear effects; sound, haptics, and Reduce Motion are separate preferences.

What to include

Please include the product name, what happened, what you expected, your device model, operating-system version, app version and build number, and any useful level, room, or session number. A screenshot is welcome, provided it does not show information you do not want to share.
