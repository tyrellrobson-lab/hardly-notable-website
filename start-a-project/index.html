Privacy before you open

Opening the inquiry window connects to Brevo, our messaging provider. We use your message and contact details to respond to your inquiry. Brevo also processes a visitor ID, browser and language information, page and referrer information, visit and page counts, timestamps and coarse location, and uses local storage. Google Analytics reporting is disabled for this widget; this does not make it tracking-free.

Sending an inquiry does not subscribe you to marketing or change an existing newsletter subscription. Inquiry notifications create separate email copies.

We review closed inquiries after 90 days and remove records we no longer need, including separate email copies. We retain necessary client records and keep newsletter consent and suppression records separate. This is a managed review, not automatic deletion. Brevo may retain information under its own policies; we cannot promise that removing a record from our account deletes every provider copy.

See Brevo’s privacy policy. For privacy questions or access, correction or deletion requests, contact hardlynotablellc@gmail.com.
