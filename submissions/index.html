Made something worth sharing?

Hardly Notable accepts original writing for possible future publication. You keep ownership of your work.

Submission does not guarantee publication. Nothing is sent from this website without you choosing to send the email.

What to include

  • Your name and preferred email address
  • The title and category of the work
  • The writing itself or a shareable link
  • An optional note about the piece

What happens next

Your email will be reviewed for possible future publication. You retain ownership, and any additional permission needed to publish accepted work will be handled separately.
